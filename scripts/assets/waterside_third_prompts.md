使用内置 ImageGen，透明 PNG 母图保存于同目录；`../generate_waterside_third_assets.py` 只裁切、缩放并适配既有分件，输入与输出哈希见同名 `.sha256.json`。刺莲沿用 Lilypad 单叶时间轴；淤泥僵尸沿用完整 ConeZombie 时间轴，保留路障、断肢、走路与啃食轨，不新增帧事件。

## 刺莲叶面

母图：`thorn_lily_source.png`；参考图：权威 `image/reanim/LilyPad_body.png`。

> Edit the reference into a game-ready Plants vs Zombies classic 2D sprite for Thorn Lily (刺莲), a powerful upgraded lily pad. Preserve the original flat oval leaf silhouette, leaf veins, small eyes low on the front-right rim, viewpoint and central open platform. Add a modest ring of chunky curved ivory-green thorn tips emerging from the leaf perimeter, and a dark teal-green thick fleshy scalloped rim. The central leaf surface must remain clear to support another plant sprite. Single isolated leaf, no stem, no pot, no water, no text, no shadow, transparent background. Hand-painted cartoon, clear dark outlines, soft simple internal shading that matches the original. Keep low flat proportions: overall width about twice height. Do not add a flower, character body, mouth, spikes in the center, or background.

## 淤泥装备

母图：`sludge_gear_source.png`。

> Create one isolated equipment assembly sprite for a Plants vs Zombies classic 2D mud-spraying zombie viewed from the side facing LEFT. An olive-brown backpack mud tank on the RIGHT, a thick flexible hose curling down from the tank and connecting to a short broad handheld resin nozzle projecting horizontally to the LEFT. The nozzle is at lower-middle height. Tank has dark straps, muddy seams, a small amber pressure gauge and wet brown mud stains. It is equipment only: absolutely no zombie, no human, no hand, no head, no road cone, no text. Transparent background. Clear classic hand-painted cartoon, thick brown outlines, rounded chunky shapes, simple dimensional highlights, muted green and brown palette. Single connected compact assembly, tank and hose with nozzle occupy one continuous silhouette. Will be composited onto the original zombie torso at game size 85px wide by 80px high. No photorealism, no background, no floor or cast shadow.

## 泥浆

母图：`sludge_splat_source.png`；弹丸与挂泥贴片使用同一母图。

> Create a single isolated thick wet brown mud splat decal for a classic Plants vs Zombies 2D hand-painted cartoon game. Transparent background. Compact irregular broad horizontal splash silhouette with seven rounded lobes and three tiny detached droplets very close to its edge, dark chocolate outline, earthy umber middle, lighter ochre streaks and two small creamy wet highlights, readable at 40 pixels wide. No face, no eyes, no text, no objects or scenery. This one sprite will be scaled into a projectile and a mud-on-plant overlay. Solid opaque painted mud, crisp anti-aliased edges with genuinely transparent exterior, no glow, no outside shadows.
